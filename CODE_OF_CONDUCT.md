# Code of conduct

Sophia follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who use Sophia. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in any project space or in public when representing the project.

**Reporting:** if someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/ds1734770-dot/Sophia/security/advisories/new) (it reaches the maintainer only; mention "conduct" in the title) instead of a public issue. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors who break this code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
