# Third-party licenses

Sophia itself is MIT licensed (see LICENSE). It depends on the packages below, installed
by pip from PyPI, and downloads the models listed at the end. Generated by
`python scripts/licenses.py` from a macOS install; Windows adds pywin32 (PSF),
comtypes (MIT) and uiautomation (Apache-2.0) and leaves out PyObjC (MIT).

## Models and data downloaded at setup

These are not in the repository; Sophia downloads them on first use.

| Model | Used for | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whisper `base.en` and `tiny.en` (OpenAI, through faster-whisper / CTranslate2) | speech recognition, and recognising "Hey Sophia" | MIT |
| Moonshine `base` and `tiny` (Useful Sensors) | fast speech recognition | MIT |
| Piper voice `en_GB-jenny_dioco-medium` | spoken replies | Piper voice: MIT. Dataset "Jenny (Dioco)": commercial use allowed, **attribution required**: the voice must be referred to as "Jenny (Dioco)" |
| openWakeWord pre-trained models (only if you set `WAKE_WORD` to one of them, such as `hey_jarvis`) | optional keyword wake word | **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial**. Not used by default |

Google Gemini is used through your own API key under Google's terms; it is a service, not a bundled component.
